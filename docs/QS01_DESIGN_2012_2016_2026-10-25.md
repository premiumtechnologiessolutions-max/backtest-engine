## Frozen implementation conventions

`quote_stress.py` requires 241 consecutive M1 observations, uses the median of the **prior** 240 close spreads, and evaluates the shock after the current M1 close. Prior ATR excludes the current shock candle. The preregistration did not spell out the Wilder seed; the convention chosen before this run was SMA20 of true ranges with a contiguous previous close, then Wilder recursion with alpha 1/20, resetting after gaps. No alternate seed was searched.

Signals are confirmed at candle-open timestamp + 60 seconds. The central market engine accepts an entry only at that exact timestamp (`max_entry_delay_minutes=0`), and never peeks at whether a next quote exists. The frozen stop and target are both 1.5 times prior ATR. Bid/Ask spread is solely a predictor: both sides execute on the same synthetic BID reference with **$0.40/oz ALL-IN roundtrip**, once, and no additional spread/commission/slippage fee.

The central optional holding cap is 30 elapsed UTC minutes **from entry**. At an observed open, an already breached stop/target is processed first; otherwise a due time exit occurs at that open, before the current candle's high/low. Missing intervals do not create an executable timer fill. Stop-first M1 ambiguity, adverse open gap-through and no same-M1 replacement remain in force.
