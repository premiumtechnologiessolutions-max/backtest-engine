## Interpretation and next experiment

This is one wick-rejection hypothesis family with 28,800 **highly correlated parameter variations**, not 28,800 independent trading systems. Data for 2012–2013 were already exposed in prior projects, so neither annual positive net nor any monthly-hit statistic is a clean OOS result. No candidate qualifies for a claim of consistent profits or live deployment. Expand next into a *different* family (e.g. multi-timeframe regime-conditioned entries), maintain frozen hypotheses and count only completed real-data replays. Test portfolio combinations with a **single shared position capacity**, not by adding independent equity curves.
