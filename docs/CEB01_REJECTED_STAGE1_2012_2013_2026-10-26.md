**CEB-M15: 9/24 positive months, -$144.48 summed separately by year, 473 trades. REJECT.**
**CEB-H1: 10/24 positive months, -$9.628 summed separately by year, 93 trades. REJECT.**
Both fail the predeclared threshold of 16/24 positive months, net positive across the two annual ledgers, and at least 50 trades in **each** year. H1 has a profitable 2013, but that is not a stable edge; the two years are independently restarted, not one continuous portfolio. No parameter adjustment is permitted under CEB-01; no stage-2 2014–2016 opening is warranted.

For both years and both variants, the reference and fast trade ledgers passed equality checks before monthly results were emitted. The observed BID M1 OHLC is synthetic reference execution, not a validated executable bid/ask/tick broker fill. Some gap-path outcomes remain uncertain; profitability cannot be inferred. Monthly positivity is on trade exits, distinct from month-end marked-to-market equity.

Private audit outputs (not in GitHub): `NoRiskFX_CEB01_Frozen_2012_2013_Negative_Control.zip`, with `year_scoreboard.json`, frozen spec, individual trade/month/week/equity ledgers. Public documentation intentionally includes no raw market bars, trade timestamps, or proprietary quote rows.

**Decision:** archive CEB-01 as a negative control and redirect next preregistered design research to a distinct family. Do not rescue these parameters through historical tuning.
