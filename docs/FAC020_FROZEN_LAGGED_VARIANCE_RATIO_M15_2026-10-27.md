## Frozen causal hypothesis

At each **completed** M15 bar `j`, estimate a lagged two-step variance ratio `VR2` from **N previous M15 close-to-close returns ending at j-1**. `VR2 = sample_var(r[k]+r[k-1], k=j-N+1..j-1) / (2*sample_var(r[k], k=j-N..j-1))`, using N-1 overlapping two-step observations. Exclude zero/invalid denominator, require at least N+1 contiguous M15 observations up to and including j, no invented bars or overnight bridging. The current bar j is never in the VR estimate. A current j directional body with absolute size >= `body_min * median(previous 20 completed M15 high-low ranges)` triggers at **j close**; ignore zero bodies and invalid/nonpositive prior ranges.

For `PERSIST` require VR2 >= 1.05 or 1.15, and follow the current completed j body direction. For `REVERT` require VR2 <= 0.95 or 0.85, and fade the current completed j body direction. `N in {8,12,20,32}`; `body_min in {0.25,0.50}`. Last **completed** H1 range / previous 24 observed H1 median range (within 168 elapsed hours) must be valid and no older than 3600 seconds at confirmation; `CALM` is (0,1], `ACTIVE` is (1,2], >2 is a causal trading halt. No 8-consecutive-H4 requirement. Sessions `ALL`, `LONDON_LOCAL_08_12`, `NY_LOCAL_0930_1330` use IANA time zones and respect DST.
